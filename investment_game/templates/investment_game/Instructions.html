Overview

In this experiment, you will make decisions about investment projects. Each round starts with an endowment of 100 tokens.

At the end of the experiment, three rounds will be randomly selected for payment. Your bonus will depend on your earnings in these three selected rounds.

Project state

Each project can be in either a good state or a bad state. You will not know the true state when making your decisions.

Signals

At the beginning of each round, you receive a public signal. The public signal can be Good or Bad.

The public signal is correct with probability 60%. This means that if the true state is good, the signal says “Good” 60% of the time. If the true state is bad, the signal says “Bad” 60% of the time.

After seeing the public signal, you can buy additional private information for 15 tokens. If you buy it, you receive a private signal, also Good or Bad.

The private signal is correct with probability 80%.

Investment

  • If you do not buy private information, you can invest up to 100 tokens.
  • If you buy private information, you can invest up to 85 tokens.
  • Tokens not invested are kept.

Payoff tables

The payoff structure may change during the experiment. When this happens, a new payoff table will be shown.

Please read the payoff table carefully before making your decision in each round.

Project survival and payments

Returns are paid over four periods. However, the project may stop before the end.

In each round, you will see a continuation probability. This is the probability that the project survives from one period to the next.

For example, if the continuation probability is 86%, then after each period there is an 86% chance that the project continues to the next period, and a 14% chance that it stops.

If the project stops after period 2, you receive the payments from periods 1 and 2, but you receive nothing from periods 3 and 4.
