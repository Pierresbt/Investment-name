Before the investment game starts, you will complete a short risk preference task.

This task is designed to study decision-making under risk.

In each row, you must choose between Option A and Option B. Each option is a lottery: it gives one payoff with some probability and another payoff with the remaining probability.

Option A is safer: the two possible payoffs are closer to each other. Option B is riskier: it can give a higher payoff, but it can also give a much lower payoff.

At the end of the experiment, one of the ten decisions below will be randomly selected and paid for real. Therefore, please make each decision carefully.

There is no right or wrong answer. Please choose the option you personally prefer in each row.
