Before the experiment starts, here is a simple example of how the investment game works.

Example project

Suppose you invest 10 tokens. The project can generate payments over four periods, but it may stop before the end.

Period Payment per token invested If you invested 10 tokens
1 2 20 tokens
2 1 10 tokens
3 1 10 tokens
4 0.5 5 tokens

If the project survives for 2 periods, you receive the payments from periods 1 and 2. You do not receive payments from periods 3 and 4.

20 + 10 = 30 tokens

Any tokens that you did not invest are also kept.

In the real experiment, the payoff table changes across rounds, and you will decide how much to invest.
